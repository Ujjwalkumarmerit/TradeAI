# Shipping and delivery
Standard domestic delivery usually takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Tracking updates may pause while a parcel moves between carrier facilities. If tracking has not changed for more than 5 business days, escalate to the delivery support team.
